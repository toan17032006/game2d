# Plan Gate

- MUST verify `prompt.md`.
- MUST verify `task.md`.
- MUST verify `spec.md`.
- MUST verify `plan.md`.
- MUST verify objective, scope, non-goals, dependencies, acceptance criteria and rollback.
- MUST verify every phase has action, output, validation and status.

## PASS

Plan and spec are complete and internally consistent.

## FAIL

Stop. Update plan/spec or switch to `CLARIFYING`.
